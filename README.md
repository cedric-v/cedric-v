## 🛠️ Product Engineering & Systems

I don't just write code; I build **frictionless paths**. As a Product Engineer, I step into the user's shoes to solve business problems with technical elegance.

### ⚡ Modern Web Development (Jamstack)
I build high-performance, secure, and eco-friendly static sites using **Cloudflare Pages** that load instantly and require zero maintenance.
- **Stack:** Nunjucks, Eleventy (11ty). I rely on Modern CSS for lean, maintainable interfaces—prioritizing performance over framework bloat. I integrate Next.js only when complex client-side interactivity is required.
- **Edge Computing:** I leverage **GitHub Actions** for automated deployments and **Cloudflare Workers** or **Bunny Edge Scripting** for dynamic logic.
- **Philosophy:** Performance as a feature. If it doesn't need a server, it shouldn't have one.

### ☁️ Low-Maintenance Infrastructure
I build "serverless-first" to focus 100% on product value. By implementing managed solutions like Supabase or Firebase, I ensure a scalable, secure architecture without the overhead of server management.
- **Managed Services:** Implementing **Supabase**, **Firebase**, or **Cloudflare D1/KV** to ensure a scalable architecture without the overhead of server management.

### 🤖 Workflow Automation (Node.js & Deno)
I automate the repetitive to free up human creativity. I specialize in identifying friction in business processes and eliminating it through code.
- **Stack:** Python, TypeScript, Google Apps Script.
- **CI/CD:** Heavy use of **GitHub Actions** to automate deployments and data flows (as seen in my [fluance-io](https://github.com/cedric-v/fluance-io) repo).

### Strategic & Process Audits
I use my marketing background to ensure every technical decision serves a business objective, focusing on Growth Hacking, conversion, and process efficiency.

## 🚀 Open Source Projects

Two products born from my own pain points as a solopreneur — built in the open, self-hostable, and designed to give you back control of your data.

### [OwnPulse](https://github.com/cedric-v/OwnPulse) — 🛰️ The pilot tool for solopreneurs
A sovereign Social CRM, marketing and financial intelligence dashboard in one private place.
- **Social CRM** — capture leads from LinkedIn, Threads & Instagram with one click (Chrome extension), manage your pipeline, and get a 360° view of each customer's history.
- **Marketing intelligence** — know exactly which channels bring your customers, measure time-to-conversion, and track offer profitability and retention.
- **CFO dashboard** — real revenue vs. expenses, net profit simulation after taxes & social contributions, and runway analysis.
- **👤 For:** solopreneurs and independents who want to pilot leads, marketing and finances in one sovereign place — without scattering their data across ten SaaS tools.

### [Studio Clarté](https://github.com/cedric-v/studio-clarte) — 🏗️ AI content studio for static sites
**Generate. Review. Preview. Publish.** — an open-source AI publishing pipeline for Astro, Eleventy or any static stack.
- **Multi-page AI generation** — one prompt, a plan, one model call per file: no truncation, everything assembled into a single PR.
- **Human-in-the-loop** — review and fine-tune every generated file in an integrated editor before anything goes live.
- **Zero risk on `main`** — always branch + PR, Cloudflare preview, real deployment tracking and one-click rollback.
- **Multi-tenant white-label** — one deployment, one studio subdomain per client, client-owned storage.
- **👤 For:** site owners who want AI-assisted content with a human final say, and agencies running a white-label content service for many clients.
